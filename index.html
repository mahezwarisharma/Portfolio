Module 01

Overview

I design learning experiences the way a good course is built: with a clear objective, a deliberate sequence, and a way to check whether it worked. Over seven years I've applied ADDIE, SAM, and Bloom's Taxonomy across three very different learner groups — ICSE computer science students, adult UPSC/UGC-NET exam aspirants, and robotics/STEM learners — and I'm now bringing that same rigor to corporate and product-facing eLearning.

What I bring: needs analysis and storyboarding, authoring in Articulate Storyline and Rise, SCORM/xAPI-aware module design, and a habit of running every module through a real pilot and revision cycle before it ships.
