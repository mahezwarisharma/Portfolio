Handling a Difficult Stakeholder Conversation

A single decision point, three ways to handle it, three different outcomes — no single choice is flagged "wrong," each shows a realistic consequence so the learner builds judgment rather than memorizes an answer key.

Learning objective: Given a stakeholder pushing back on a project timeline, the learner can choose a response that keeps the relationship and the project on track.
